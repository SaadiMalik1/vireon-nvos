# Hardware Digital Twins

Digital Twins simulate the imperfections of physical EEG/BCI hardware. Instead of assuming perfect signal acquisition, VIREON digital twins inject thermal noise, amplifier saturation, and finite-precision quantization, rigorously testing how an algorithm survives on low-cost vs. clinical-grade devices.

## Scientific Rigor in Phase E
As of Phase E, this scientific principle is fully integrated into the framework. Methods are evaluated dynamically against real-world and adversarial conditions, ensuring that claims made about algorithmic efficiency are backed by cryptographic execution reports.
