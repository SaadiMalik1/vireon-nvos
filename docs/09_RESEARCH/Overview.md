# Ongoing Research

VIREON is not just a software engineering project; it is an active scientific research endeavor.

## Active Research Vectors

1. **Patient-Specific Head Modeling:** Moving beyond standard MNI templates. We are researching methods to rapidly generate finite element models (FEM) from individual patient MRIs to improve the precision of the `vireon-models` forward projections.
2. **Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) for Artifacts:** Replacing the deterministic mathematical artifact models with GANs trained on large clinical datasets to generate highly realistic, unpredictable physiological noise.
3. **Ontological Expansion:** Continuously mapping new mathematical assumptions into the `vireon-knowledge` JSON-LD graph as novel signal processing techniques emerge in literature.
